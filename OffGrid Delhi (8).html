The concept

Build the game.

250 students get a theme and a deadline. Make a playable game with mentors for design, art and code, then present it to a jury. No experience needed.

Solve the city.

The top 50 decode a cryptic trail across Delhi Metro stations and landmarks. Daytime only, and every team travels with an adult chaperone.

Clue zero

The hunt starts before the jam does. Somebody scrambled a Delhi Metro interchange. Turn the wheel until the letters make sense, then type the station name.

iHelp point

Questions

Who can take part?

Students under 18 living or studying in Delhi NCR. Every participant needs a parent or guardian's consent.

Do I need game development experience?

No. Beginners are welcome, and mentors will be there to help. Tools and platforms will be shared before the jam.

Can I join as a team?

Team size and rules will be published with registration.

Is the metro hunt safe?

Yes, by design. Teams travel with an adult chaperone, in daytime, on fixed routes, with check-ins at every stop.

Does it cost anything?

Any fees will be listed on the registration page before you sign up.

What can we win?

Prizes, scholarships and equipment from our partners, plus merch for every participant. Details will be announced.
